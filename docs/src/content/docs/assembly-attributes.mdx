---
title: Assembly Attributes
description: Apply assembly-level attributes to your source generator for polyfills.
---

There are assembly-level attributes that can be applied to your source generator assembly to enable polyfills for certain features:

- **EnableRecordSupport** - Enables support for C# 9 [record types](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/builtin-types/record) in your source generator assembly
- **EnableRequiredSupport** - Enables support for C# 11 [required members](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/csharp/language-reference/keywords/required) in your source generator assembly

When applied, they will emit polyfills for the features in your source generator assembly.

```csharp title="AssemblyInfo.cs"
using Hertzole.SourceGen;

[assembly: EnableRecordSupport]
[assembly: EnableRequiredSupport]
```
